Caster Level is often lower than Character Level — a Paladin/Ranger's is Character Level − 3 (starting at 4th level), for example. Set it independently; it pre-fills spell entries below that scale with caster level (Divine Favor, Magic Weapon Greater, Wrath, Gorum's Armor...) instead of Character Level.

Ability Scores

Character

Character note only — doesn't hide or filter anything. Feats/traits from any sourcebook are always shown; check their own prerequisites to see if your character actually qualifies.
Deity-specific catalog entries (a handful of feats/traits/spells tied to worshipping one particular god) only show up once you've picked that deity here. Leave empty to see none of those — everything else is unaffected.

Weapon

Pick whichever ability your build actually uses for each roll — they don't have to match, and you can pick the same one for both (e.g. Weapon Finesse: Dex to attack, Str to damage; Agile/Slashing Grace: Dex to both; a bow: Dex to attack, none to damage; a thrown weapon: Str to both). The calculator doesn't check weapon-by-weapon eligibility, so choose what fits your character.

Off-Hand Weapon

Attack penalties follow the standard two-weapon-fighting table (-6/-10 normal, -4/-4 light) and are automatically reduced if you add the "Two-Weapon Fighting" feat below; off-hand damage uses half your damage-ability bonus (from "Ability → Damage rolls" above) unless "Double Slice" is active.

Ammunition

RAW: a projectile weapon's own enhancement bonus (above) only helps overcome damage reduction — it is NOT added to attack or damage rolls. The bonus that IS added comes from the ammunition's own enhancement bonus, set here. Weapon Effects below (Flaming, Keen, Bane, etc.) can be tagged "Ammunition Only" / "Bow/Crossbow Only" for your own bookkeeping — informational only, since a ranged attack only produces one attack/damage total either way.

Target

Oozes, elementals, swarms, and incorporeal creatures are immune to critical hits and precision damage (Sneak Attack, etc.) in PF1e — picking one of those four automatically zeroes out Sneak Attack/Precise Strike above and flags Critical Damage below as not applicable. Constructs and (non-incorporeal) undead are NOT immune to crits/precision damage in PF1e, unlike 3.5e — don't gate those the same way.
Modifiers that only work against a specific enemy type (Favored Enemy, Bane, Challenge Evil, etc.) check this — set it before a fight and they'll switch on/off automatically. Leave it on "none" and they behave as before (always on while active).
