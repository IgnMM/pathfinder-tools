Companion & Attacks

Feeds Power Attack's BAB-based scaling only — each attack below has its own Base To-Hit field. For sources with a known progression table, Hit Dice and BAB are pre-filled automatically; type over either to take manual control (use "Reapply progression" above to go back to auto-fill).
A species preset fills the Core Rulebook starting scores and attacks, including its 4th/7th-level advancement when reached. Everything remains manually editable afterward.

Evolutions

Check this only if the companion is making a single natural attack this turn (not a full attack with multiple attack types) — like a two-handed weapon, its Strength (and Power Attack) bonus to damage then becomes ×1.5 instead of ×1. Leave unchecked for the common case (e.g. bite + 2 claws together).

Ability Scores

Almost every companion uses Strength for both. Only change this for an unusual build (e.g. Dex-to-damage via a feat/feature that specifically grants it to natural attacks).

Attacks

Presets only fill editable attack rows; they never lock the creature to a species. Base To-Hit is the attack's BAB/size/other built-in value before its selected ability and active effects. Base Damage is a fixed amount before its selected ability and effects. Damage dice and damage type are also editable. A creature with 3+ natural attacks and Multiattack takes only -2 on secondary attacks instead of -5; enter that difference in Base To-Hit when needed.

Modifiers — pick from the catalog per category, or add a custom one

Uses the shared Character Calculator catalog, filtered by the selected entity. Entries with an automatic adapter calculate immediately; the rest remain editable informational/custom effects. Poison, AC and saving-throw effects are shown only as notes when relevant.

Active Effects

Shared effects apply to every eligible attack. Attack-specific evolutions and manually targeted effects remain attached to their individual attack rows.
