Attacks

One row per attack type (Claw 1, Claw 2, Bite, Tail Slap...). "Attack Bonus" is BAB + size, typed directly — Strength and every active Feat/Spell/Other Effect below are added automatically. "Primary" attacks (the creature's main natural weapon type, or its only attack) use full Strength bonus to damage; "Secondary" attacks (extra attack types that come with a primary, like a bite alongside claws) use half — see Combat rules on natural attacks. A creature with 3+ natural attacks and the Multiattack feat only takes a -2 penalty on secondary attacks instead of -5 — bake that into each line's Attack Bonus by hand.
