Spiritualists are spontaneous casters like sorcerers: they know a fixed number of spells (the "Known" toggle), then cast any known spell of a level using that level's daily slot pool. Orisons (0-level) are known the same way but cast at will, unlimited. Uses Wisdom for bonus spells and DCs; caster level equals spiritualist level.
